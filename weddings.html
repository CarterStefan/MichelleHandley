01

The ceremony

Walking in to a song that means something to you both is a moment you’ll never forget. I’ll sing as guests arrive and take their seats, for your entrance, while you sign the register, and as you leave as a married couple.

  • Length 30 to 45 minutes
  • Covers Arrivals, entrance, signing, exit
  • Style Classic, modern and classical
02

The drinks reception

Relaxed, feel-good covers while you’re having photos taken and your guests are mingling over a glass of something sparkling. Lively enough to lift the mood, gentle enough that everyone can still chat.

  • Length Two 45-minute sets
  • Covers Drinks, canapés, photos
  • Style Acoustic pop, soul, jazz standards
03

The evening and your first dance

Your first dance, sung live, just for the two of you. I can follow it with an evening set of singalong favourites to fill the dance floor before your DJ or band takes over.

  • Length First dance plus up to 60 minutes
  • Covers First dance, evening party
  • Style Love songs, party classics

Questions

Wedding FAQs

How far in advance should we book?

Most couples book 9 to 18 months ahead, especially for Saturdays between May and September. Midweek and winter dates are often free at shorter notice, so it’s always worth asking.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes. I bring a professional, venue-friendly sound system, microphones and everything needed to play high-quality backing tracks. All I need is a standard power socket near where I’m singing. If your venue has a sound limiter, I’ll work within it.

Can you learn a song that isn’t on your list?

Absolutely. Every package includes at least one newly learned song. Extra requests are [PLACEHOLDER: price] each, with at least [PLACEHOLDER: number] weeks’ notice.

Do you travel?

Happily. Travel within [PLACEHOLDER: distance] of [PLACEHOLDER: location] is included. Beyond that I charge [PLACEHOLDER: travel rate], and for weddings further afield I’ll include travel and accommodation in your quote.

Can you sing outdoors?

Yes, as long as there’s a covered spot for the equipment and access to power. We’ll agree a wet-weather plan together so there are no surprises on the day.

Can you perform with live musicians?

I sing to high-quality backing tracks as standard. If you’d love live accompaniment, I work with a guitarist and a pianist I trust and can include them in your quote. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm musicians]

What happens if you’re ill on the day?
