I grew up in a house that was never quiet. There was always a radio on, someone at the piano, and a family party that ended in a singalong. I joined my first choir at eight, sang my first solo at twelve, and never really stopped.

After training at [PLACEHOLDER: training], I spent [PLACEHOLDER: number] years performing in [PLACEHOLDER: venues, shows or bands]. It was a wonderful education in reading a room, and in how much a single song can mean to the people listening.

Watching her walk down the aisle to the song her late grandmother used to sing, I realised this was the kind of singing I wanted to do.
