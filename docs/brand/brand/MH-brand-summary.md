This file is kept for the parts that still apply: the business (section 1), photo direction and the shot list (section 7) and voice (section 8). ## 1. The business

- **Who:** Michelle Handley, singer and singing teacher in Stafford, UK.
- **What:** singing lessons in person in Stafford and online, in pop and musical theatre. Wedding singing will be added later under the same name.
- **Who it's for:**
  - nervous adult beginners
  - children and teens, usually booked by a parent
  - students preparing for graded exams, school shows or auditions
- **How people find her:** Instagram, local search (Google), and flyers in schools, music shops and on noticeboards.
- **Personality:** warm, encouraging and down-to-earth, with a performer's polish. Friendly, sweet, approachable.
- **Not:** a children's party entertainer, a music-school franchise, or a luxury bridal brand.

## 2. The idea: Green Room

The green room is where performers wait and settle their nerves before they step up to the mic. Lessons with Michelle are that room. Her initials make the mark:

- the M forms the top of a stage microphone's grille, and the H its sides and bars
- footlight yellow glows inside
- the colours come from backstage: stage-door green, dressing-room pink, footlight yellow

## 5. Type

Both fonts are free for commercial use, on Google Fonts and in Canva.

- **Josefin Sans** (display): a geometric 1930s-style sans, from the era of the classic stage mic, whose even strokes match the mic's lines.
  - Bold 700: headlines and her name.
  - SemiBold 600: labels in capitals, letter spacing 0.24em (240 in Canva).
  - The logo line is spaced 0.4em.
- **Lato** (reading): warm and clear at small sizes.
  - Regular 400: body text and captions.
  - Bold 700: prices, times and the odd key phrase.

**Scale**

| Role | Social (1080 px) | Website | Print |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | 96–120 px | 44–80 px | 54–90 pt |
| Headline | 64–80 | 32–48 px | 24–36 pt |
| Subhead | 40–48 | 22–34 px | 14–18 pt |
| Body | 32–36 | 18 px | 10–12 pt |
| Label | 22–28 | 14 px | 8–9 pt |
| Caption, small print | 26 at least | 14–16 px | 8 pt at least; 7 pt on the business card |

**Rules**

- **Headlines:**
  - sentence case
  - line spacing 1.0–1.05, letter spacing −0.01em at large sizes
  - about eight words at most
- **Labels:** one to four words. They introduce; they never carry the message.
- **Body:**
  - line spacing 1.5–1.6
  - ranged left, never justified
  - 45–75 characters a line on the web
- **Order:** label, headline, body, then one action, the same everywhere.
- **Josefin's position:** it sits high in its box, so nudge it down about 0.12em when centring it in pills and buttons.

## 6. Graphic devices and layout

- **The m-arch edge:** the M's arches in a row, along one top or bottom edge, starting at the margin.
  - Arch width is 2 × the radius; line weight 0.4 × the radius; round ends.
  - Yellow on green, or green on pink or white.
  - At most one per layout.
  - In carousels it may run across the slides to pull the eye on.
- **The arch frame:** one arch of the M used as a doorway for portraits, with a flat bottom on a page or panel edge. In Canva, search Frames for "arch".
- **The footlight pill:**
  - yellow, fully rounded, Ink text in Josefin Sans Bold
  - about 2.5 × the text height tall
  - at most one per layout, for the one action that matters
- **The green-room split:** a green upper panel for the promise, a pink lower panel for who, where, when and how much. The pill sits on the seam.
- **Margins:**
  - 84 px on a 1080 px post
  - 12 mm inside the trim on A5 and A4
  - 4 mm on the business card
  - 24 px on phones, 64 px on desktops
- **Spacing steps:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96.
- **Text on photos:** use a solid green or pink band, or the photo's calm third. No gradient fades, no blurred glass boxes, no text shadows.

## 7. Photo and video

- **Light and colour:**
  - window light, warm and natural
  - backgrounds: a deep green wall or curtain, plants, or a plain light wall
  - solid clothes in green, blush, mustard or white
  - no blue light, beauty filters or heavy presets
- **Framing:**
  - shoot everything twice, upright for Instagram and sideways for the website
  - use the 1× or 2× lens at eye level
  - leave a calm third of the frame for text
- **Video:**
  - upright 9:16, with the phone propped steady
  - record the sound close to the singer
  - captions always on
- **Children and consent:**
  - No child's face anywhere without a parent's written consent for that exact use.
  - By default, show students from behind, as hands on a lyric sheet, as a silhouette, or in a wide shot where no one can be recognised.
  - Never name a child together with their school.
  - Every template works with no photo at all.
- **First shoot, 12 shots, about an hour:**
  1. A portrait by a window.
  2. The same portrait sideways, with space on the left.
  3. Singing, mid-phrase.
  4. Showing a warm-up.
  5. Teaching an adult, seen from behind them.
  6. With a young student, from behind, with consent.
  7. The lesson room, wide.
  8. The online set-up.
  9. Details: the music stand, pencilled breath marks, a mug of tea.
  10. Performing, warmly lit, from the audience.
  11. Dressed up, in a venue or garden, kept for the wedding page later.
  12. Three 15-second upright clips: a warm-up, a tip to camera and a chorus.

## 8. Voice

Like a friend who happens to be a pro:
- short sentences and plain words
- written as "I"
- encouraging, never gushing

**Sample lines:**
- "Every voice starts somewhere."
- "Never sung in front of anyone? Plenty of my students hadn't. We start gently, with songs you already love."
- "Confidence first, technique close behind, built around the pop and musical theatre songs they actually want to sing."
- "Graded exams, school shows and drama school auditions, prepared step by step."
- "Ready when you are. Tell me a little about yourself, or your child, and what you'd love to sing. No experience needed."

**Banned visuals:**
- treble clefs, floating notes, piano-key borders, or microphone silhouettes other than the logo
- purple-to-blue gradients, gradient text, glows, glassmorphism
- cream or beige backgrounds by default
- the fonts Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk and Instrument Serif

## 10. Weddings

The mic, palette and fonts stay. What changes:

- The line under her name becomes "Wedding Singer", in Josefin Regular with spacing 500.
- Blush and white lead, with green for type. Yellow appears only inside the mic.
- Headlines are Josefin SemiBold, centred, with spacing +10.
- One fine arch (2 px at A5) around a white panel, like a chapel doorway, replaces the m-arch edge and the pill.
- Photos: evening light, venue details, couples from behind.
- Sample line: "A voice for the moments you'll remember."
