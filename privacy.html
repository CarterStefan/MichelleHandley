Who I am

This website is run by Michelle Handley, a singer and singing teacher based in Stafford. I am responsible for any personal information you share here. You can contact me about privacy at [EMAIL].

What I collect

I only collect what you choose to send me through the forms on this website or by email. Depending on the form, that can include:

  • your name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number
  • who the lessons are for, whether you’d like them in person or online, when suits you, and what you’d love to sing
  • anything else you write in your message
  • that you’re happy for me to reply to you

If you’re booking lessons for someone under 18, please fill in the form as their parent or guardian.

How I use it

I use your details to reply to your enquiry, to arrange lessons and, if you book, to plan and deliver what we’ve agreed. I don’t add you to a mailing list, and I never sell or share your information for marketing.

My lawful basis is that you’ve asked me to respond to you and that the information is needed to take steps towards, or carry out, a booking.
