What I collect

I only collect what you choose to send me through the enquiry forms or by email. Depending on the form, that can include:

  • your name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number
  • wedding details such as the date, venue, guest numbers and the parts of the day you’re interested in

If you’re booking lessons for someone under 18, please fill in the form as their parent or guardian.

How I use it

I use your details to reply to your enquiry, to prepare a quote or arrange lessons, and, if you book, to plan and deliver what we’ve agreed. I don’t add you to a mailing list, and I never sell or share your information for marketing.

My lawful basis is that you’ve asked me to respond to you and that the information is needed to take steps towards, or carry out, a booking.
